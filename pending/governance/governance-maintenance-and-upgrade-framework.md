# Governance Maintenance & Upgrade Framework

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how governance documents are:

* maintained
* updated
* evolved over time

---

## 3. Document Hierarchy

The Company's document hierarchy is defined in **Operating Agreement §12.5** and restated constitutionally in **Charter §13**. This Framework is an operational policy and is subordinate to both.

### 3.1 Legal Formation Documents

The Formation Documents (Operating Agreement, Certificate of Formation, BOIR Template, Asset Transfer Agreement, IP Schedule, and Continuity Statement) are legal instruments filed with the Registered Agent. They may be amended only by the process specified in **Operating Agreement Article X**:

* **Constitutional Amendments** (≥66% YES, ≥10% quorum) — changes to Articles I (Formation), II (Purpose), III (Continuity), IV (Membership and Governance Participation), XI (Disputes and Liability), XIII (Transition Period), and changes to the Charter
* **Governance Amendments** (≥60% YES, ≥7% quorum) — changes to Articles V (Delegated Functions) through XII (General Provisions)

---

## 4. Types of Changes

---

### 4.1 Patch Changes

* cosmetic or non-semantic
* do not affect meaning (e.g., typo corrections, formatting, cross-reference updates)

Approval:

* **Governance Process proposal** — standard threshold (≥60% YES, ≥7% quorum)

---

### 4.2 Minor Changes

* process improvements
* parameter adjustments
* non-structural updates to policy language

Approval:

* **Governance Process proposal** — standard threshold (≥60% YES, ≥7% quorum)

---

### 4.3 Major Changes

* changes to the Charter
* changes to governance structure
* changes affecting authority, rights, or the role of any governance body

Approval:

* **Constitutional proposal** — supermajority threshold (≥66% YES, ≥10% quorum)

All thresholds are defined in DAO Parameters §3.2 and §3.3.

---

## 5. Amendment Process

All changes must:

* be proposed through governance
* include clear rationale
* specify change type (patch / minor / major)
* include version updates

---

## 6. Versioning

Each update must include:

* version number (format: vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH — increment the appropriate segment per the change type in §4)
* summary of changes
* effective date

Version numbers referenced in Operating Agreement §10.2 follow this format.

---

## 7. Consistency Rules

Changes must:

* not conflict with higher-level documents
* maintain internal consistency across policies
* clearly state any dependencies

---

## 8. Maintenance Responsibility

* Governance & Legal Working Group maintains documents
* RAC ensures process compliance
* DAO approves all substantive changes

---

## 9. Emergency Amendments

Emergency amendments to governance documents follow the procedure defined in **Operating Agreement §10.3**: unanimous written consent of all Delegates, subject to GP ratification within 30 days. Amendments not ratified within 30 days are void. The Emergency & Safeguards Policy applies where an emergency action is required before a written consent process can be completed.

---

## 10. Transparency

All changes must be:

* publicly visible
* clearly documented
* traceable over time

---

## 11. Amendments

Changes to this framework follow the same process defined within it. A change that affects amendment thresholds or the document hierarchy is a Major Change (§4.3) and requires a Constitutional proposal.
