# Certificate of Formation — Radix DAO LLC

> **Status:** Working draft for Marshall Islands Registrar filing.
> To be executed by the incorporator(s) and filed with MIDAO Directory Services, Inc.

---

## Article I — Name

The name of the Limited Liability Company is:

**Radix DAO LLC**

---

## Article II — Entity Type

The Company is organized as a **Non-Profit Decentralized Autonomous Organization Limited Liability Company** under:

* the Marshall Islands Limited Liability Company Act (52 MIRC Ch. 3)
* the Marshall Islands DAO Act (52 MIRC Ch. 7)
* the Marshall Islands Non-Profit Entities Act (2020)

---

## Article III — Purpose

The Company is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes, including:

* Education in decentralized ledger technology and its applications
* Advancement of cryptographic systems and open-source software protocols
* Research and governance of distributed and decentralized networks
* Support for token-based participation and distributed governance mechanisms

No part of the net earnings of the Company shall inure to the benefit of, or be distributable to, any Member, Delegate, Governance Participant, or other individual, except for the permitted exceptions set out in the Operating Agreement §2.2.

---

## Article IV — Registered Agent

The name and address of the registered agent for service of process in the Republic of the Marshall Islands is:

**MIDAO Directory Services, Inc.**
Majuro, Republic of the Marshall Islands

---

## Article V — Management

The Company is algorithmically managed under §708 of the Marshall Islands DAO Act, as further described in the Operating Agreement. Governance authority is exercised by the Governance Participants through the on-chain governance system identified in Article VII. Operational functions are performed by Delegates elected by Governance Proposal as set forth in the Operating Agreement.

The Company has no managers, directors, officers, or trustees within the meaning of traditional corporate law. Binding decisions of the Company are made by the Governance Participants through the Governance Platform and are recognized by the Operating Agreement as legally binding regardless of whether participating persons hold formal Membership.

---

## Article VI — Duration

The Company has perpetual duration unless dissolved in accordance with the Operating Agreement or by operation of law.

---

## Article VII — Smart Contract Identifiers

The smart contract(s) and account(s) through which the Company's governance and treasury are conducted — including the governance smart-contract component and the treasury multi-signature account — are maintained in the **On-Chain Identifiers & Verification Policy** forming part of the Company's governance framework. That register is published to the Company's Official Venue, carries a public explorer verification link for each identifier, and is the authoritative operational record referenced by Operating Agreement §11.4.

The identifiers are recorded with the Registered Agent and are updated in accordance with that policy and Operating Agreement §11.4. Where required by section 106(2) or section 107 of the Marshall Islands DAO Act, the Company shall procure an amendment to this Certificate to reflect any updated identifier.

---

## Article VIII — Member Rights Notice

> **Notice required by Marshall Islands DAO Act:**
> The rights of members of this Company may differ materially from the rights of members in other limited liability companies. The Operating Agreement may provide that fiduciary duties owed between members, and between members and the Company, may be defined, reduced, or eliminated. Transfer restrictions on membership interests may apply. Membership is limited to admitted persons in accordance with the Operating Agreement; participation in on-chain governance does not, by itself, confer Membership. Prospective members and participants are advised to review the Operating Agreement carefully before participating in governance.

---

## Article IX — Non-Distribution Statement

The Company is organized on a non-distribution basis. Upon dissolution, remaining assets must be transferred to an entity meeting the qualifying criteria set out in the Operating Agreement §9.2. No assets may be distributed to Members, Delegates, or Governance Participants upon dissolution.
